Branch ranking

Branch scores and ranking
Ranks are calculated within the selected reporting month, GOV and branch status. Selecting one branch retains its rank in that scope. Equal totals rounded to two decimals share a rank (1, 1, 3). Change is a score-point difference against the previous calendar month for the same branch. Provisional and closed branches remain visible without a rank. Ratings use the agreed score bands. YTD score is the average of available complete monthly scores from January through the selected month; the number of scored months is shown.
